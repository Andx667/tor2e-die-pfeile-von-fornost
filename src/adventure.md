## Overview {#overview .nonotes}

One paragraph that sells the adventure: who the Company is, what they are asked to do, what they find, and what they have to decide at the end.

**Setting:** Region (*Eriador*), season, TA year.

**Mood:** Two or three words.

**Length:** One session of about … hours, a single *Adventuring Phase*. Written for 3–5 heroes, ideally four.

**Rules:** Terms and page references follow the English *Core Rules* of *The One Ring, 2nd Edition*.
